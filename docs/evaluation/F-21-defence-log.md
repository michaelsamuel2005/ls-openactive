**Rule:** a defence topic is not closed by having a good answer written down. It is closed by a
dated result — `defended`, `partial` or `not-yet` — recorded against the topic by a named
non-author examiner. Anything short of `defended` requires a re-test, also dated.

## Result definitions

- **`defended`** — explained without notes to a named non-author examiner, including the
  strongest objection to the position, and the examiner recorded it as satisfactory.
- **`partial`** — the main line held but a follow-up exposed a gap. Record the gap. Re-test required.
- **`not-yet`** — not attempted, or did not hold. Re-test required.

## Evidence retained per topic (work package §21)

Issue and acceptance criteria; dated rationale and rejected alternatives; source evidence; scoped
PR; tests; generated outputs; non-author review and disposition; change history; AI assistance and
human verification; claim and decision links.

Commit count, line count and nominal co-authorship are explicitly **not** evidence.
